## Meta restriction safeguards (the hard part of this plan)
The page's earlier restriction is the thing we do not repeat. These rules are binding for whoever schedules:
1. **Cadence cap: 3 posts/day, never more** — the page's proven rhythm since Oct 27. No catch-up dumps, no double-posting a slot.
2. **Fixed slots only: 12:00 / 18:30 / 21:00 Asia/Manila** — hours apart, human-looking rhythm. Never schedule two posts minutes apart.
3. **Schedule through Meta Business Suite's own scheduler, in weekly batches** — no third-party bulk API posting scripts. (The repo's scheduler scripts stay untouched on Muse's box — standing rule.)
4. **Every caption unique** — written per video from its own story (copy packs roll weekly, Week 1 attached). No copy-paste captions, no repeated text blocks across posts.
5. **No external links in caption bodies.** YouTube pointers go in the pinned comment, posted after the reel is live.
6. **No duplicate uploads** — each slug appears exactly once in this grid (audit is the source of truth; the Amalanhig double-post does not repeat). Antigravity marks files DONE- as they are scheduled.
7. **Caption hygiene for graphic topics** — atmospheric wording, no gore vocabulary in captions (the videos themselves are unchanged; this is about the text Meta scans).
8. **No engagement bait** — no "share or else", no tag-begging. Questions in pinned comments only.
9. **IG stays on its own rhythm** — the same video never lands on FB and IG in the same minute; IG mirrors at most 1/day, next day, using `.ig-optimized` cuts where they exist (ninja-poison-and-disguises has one ready).
10. **Stop rule** — if Meta shows any warning (reach throttle notice, content flag, account quality alert), scheduling pauses and Christian is told the same day. Account Quality gets checked before each weekly batch is armed.
