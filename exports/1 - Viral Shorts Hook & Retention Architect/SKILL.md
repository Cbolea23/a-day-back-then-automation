# Viral Shorts Hook & Retention Architect

This skill governs audience watch-time optimization, swipe-away prevention, and storytelling momentum for short-form vertical video (YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels) and fast-paced horizontal content.

---

## 1. The Core Psychology of Short-Form Retention

Viewers decide whether to stay or swipe away within **400 to 800 milliseconds**. 
Traditional video introductions (logos, greetings, rhetorical questions) guarantee a >70% swipe-away rate.

### 🚫 The Banned Openings (Never Use)
- "Hey guys, welcome back to my channel..."
- "In today's video, we are going to talk about..."
- "Have you ever wondered why..."
- "Did you know that..."
- "Make sure to like and subscribe..."

### ✅ The 4 High-Conversion Cold Open Frameworks

#### Framework A: The Visceral Contradiction
Drop the viewer into a sensory contradiction where the established premise violates common sense.
- *Template:* `[Normal situation] + [Shocking or disgusting detail] + [Unfair consequence]`
- *Example (History):* "If you lived in ancient Rome, you drank spoiled fish guts every single morning—and you paid double for the privilege."
- *Example (Tech):* "Every single Wi-Fi router sold this year contains a secret radio channel that your phone connects to without your permission."

#### Framework B: The High-Stakes Threat / Urgent Warning
Instantly create personal jeopardy for the viewer.
- *Template:* `[Warning imperative] + [Everyday action] + [Immediate consequence]`
- *Example (True Crime/Folklore):* "If you smell sweet pipe tobacco in an empty forest after sunset... do not look up into the trees."
- *Example (Fitness):* "Stop stretching your hamstrings before lifting heavy—you are actually turning off your body's primary knee stabilizers."

#### Framework C: The Impossible Secret / Leaked Insider Fact
Position the viewer as an insider receiving restricted knowledge.
- *Template:* `[Authority figure] + [Hidden reality] + [Specific physical proof]`
- *Example (Aviation):* "Commercial airline pilots are strictly forbidden from eating the same meal before a flight—and the reason involves a 1982 disaster."

#### Framework D: The Extreme Escalation
Start with an ordinary baseline that spirals out of control in 5 seconds.
- *Template:* `[Small mistake] + [Absurd chain reaction] + [Current reality]`
- *Example (Finance):* "In 1637, a Dutch sailor mistook a rare tulip bulb for an onion, sliced it up for breakfast, and accidentally ate a fortune larger than his entire ship."

## 2. Cultural & Regional Sensory Anchors (Taglish / Folklore)

Generic international hooks often struggle with regional algorithmic pushes. Using culturally specific sensory anchors multiplies comment engagement by 3x:
- *Philippine Folklore Hook:* "Kung may maamoy kang tabako sa tabi ng balete tree pagkatapos ng ulan... huwag kang lilingon."
- *Acoustic Cadence:* Blend English stakes with native phrasing (*"According to local elders..."*, *"pero mag-ingat ka..."*).

---

## 3. First-3-Second Acoustic Hook Coupling

A great written hook fails if delivered flatly. The voice must acoustically drop into the hook:
1. **Dramatic Inhalation Pause:** Place ellipses (`...`) right before the core contradiction (*"You think this tree is empty... but look closer"*).
2. **Pitch & Velocity Calibration:**
   - Warning Hooks: Lower pitch by `-4Hz` and slow speed by `-5%` to create visceral dread.
   - Escalation Hooks: Elevate pitch by `+1Hz` and increase speed by `+4%` to project breaking urgency.

---

## 4. The 3.5-Second Sensory Pattern Interrupt Rule

The human brain drops attention after ~3.5 seconds of static visual stimuli.
Every scene must trigger at least one sensory shift every 3–4 seconds:
1. **Camera Motion Inversion:** Zoom-in alternates into pull-back.
2. **Character Reaction Change:** From calm explanation to recoil or panic.
3. **Kinetic Caption Color Pop:** Active syllable highlights (`#FFD93B` or `#38BDF8`).
4. **Sound Bed Shift:** Sub-bass drop, whoosh transition, or ambient ducking.

## 5. The 100-Point Viral Retention Audit Rubric

When prompted to *"Audit this script"* or *"Review retention"*, output this exact scorecard:

| Dimension | Max Points | Evaluation Criteria |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Cold Open Velocity** | 25 | Under 8 words, zero greeting fluff, triggers immediate curiosity in <800ms. |
| **Sensory Specificity** | 25 | Concrete smells, dates, amounts, or visceral physical stakes (not abstract ideas). |
| **Pattern Interrupt Density** | 25 | Camera drift, visual shift, or sound punctuation at least once every 3.5 seconds. |
| **Loop Bridge & Acoustic Cadence**| 25 | Final line syntactically flows into Scene 1 hook without a silent gap or outro card. |

**Scoring Scale:**
- **90–100:** Viral Tier (Ready to render).
- **70–89:** High Risk of Drop-off (Needs hook tightening).
- **<70:** Swipe-Away Guaranteed (Complete rewrite required).
