# Viral Shorts Hook & Retention Architect

This skill governs audience watch-time optimization, swipe-away prevention, and storytelling momentum for short-form vertical video (YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels) and fast-paced horizontal content.

---

## 1. The Core Psychology of Short-Form Retention

Viewers decide whether to stay or swipe away within **400 to 800 milliseconds**. 
Traditional video introductions (logos, greetings, rhetorical questions) guarantee a >70% swipe-away rate.

### 🚫 The Banned Openings (Never Use)
- "Hey guys, welcome back to my channel..."
- "In today's video, we are going to talk about..."
- "Have you ever wondered why..."
- "Did you know that..."
- "Make sure to like and subscribe..."

### ✅ The 4 High-Conversion Cold Open Frameworks

#### Framework A: The Visceral Contradiction
Drop the viewer into a sensory contradiction where the established premise violates common sense.
- *Template:* `[Normal situation] + [Shocking or disgusting detail] + [Unfair consequence]`
- *Example (History):* "If you lived in ancient Rome, you drank spoiled fish guts every single morning—and you paid double for the privilege."
- *Example (Tech):* "Every single Wi-Fi router sold this year contains a secret radio channel that your phone connects to without your permission."
- *Example (Finance):* "The richest families in America legally avoid inheritance tax using a 90-year-old loophole called a Dynasty Trust."

#### Framework B: The High-Stakes Threat / Urgent Warning
Instantly create personal jeopardy for the viewer.
- *Template:* `[Warning imperative] + [Everyday action] + [Immediate consequence]`
- *Example (True Crime/Folklore):* "If you smell sweet pipe tobacco in an empty forest after sunset, do not look up into the trees."
- *Example (Fitness):* "Stop stretching your hamstrings before lifting heavy—you are actually turning off your body's primary knee stabilizers."

#### Framework C: The Impossible Secret / Leaked Insider Fact
Position the viewer as an insider receiving restricted knowledge.
- *Template:* `[Authority figure] + [Hidden reality] + [Specific physical proof]`
- *Example (Aviation):* "Commercial airline pilots are strictly forbidden from eating the same meal before a flight—and the reason involves a 1982 disaster."

#### Framework D: The Extreme Escalation
Start with an ordinary baseline that spirals out of control in 5 seconds.
- *Template:* `[Small mistake] + [Absurd chain reaction] + [Current reality]`
- *Example (Business):* "A single typo on a Japanese stock trade in 2005 cost a broker \$225 million in less than 60 seconds."

## 2. The 3.5-Second Pattern Interrupt Formula

The human brain drops dopamine and attention after **3.5 seconds** of unbroken visual or audio stimuli.
Every short-form script and scene direction MUST enforce a sensory shift every 3 to 4 seconds:

1. **Camera Motion Inversion:**
   - Scene 1: Slow push-in (`scale 1.0 -> 1.15`).
   - Scene 2: Immediate snap cut to pull-back (`scale 1.15 -> 1.02`).
   - Scene 3: Lateral pan drift with atmospheric particle shift.
2. **Dynamic Kinetic Typography:**
   - Maximum 3 to 4 words on screen simultaneously.
   - Active word pop: Scale `1.0 -> 1.15` on spoken syllable with vibrant accent color (`#FFD93B` or `#38BDF8`).
3. **Sound Design Interrupt:**
   - High-impact whoosh transition or bass drop on scene cut.
   - Rhythmic sync between dialogue cadence and visual text pop.

---

## 3. The Seamless Infinite Loop (Algorithm Booster)

Shorts algorithms heavily reward **Audience Retention > 100%** (when viewers watch the video more than once).
To trigger infinite replays:

1. **Eliminate All Outros:**
   - Never say "Thanks for watching", "Follow for more", or "See you next time".
2. **Syntactic Bridge:**
   - The final spoken sentence must connect grammatically or conceptually into the very first word of Scene 1.
   - *Example Final Sentence:* "...and that is why elder villagers will always warn you:"
   - *Loops Seamlessly Into Scene 1:* "DO NOT LOOK UP INTO THE BALETE TREE."

---

## 4. Pacing & Syllable Metrics

- **Optimal Words Per Minute (WPM):** 145 – 165 WPM.
- **Shorts Target Length:** 45 – 58 seconds (5 to 6 scenes maximum).
- **Scene Duration Limit:** 7 to 11 seconds per scene. Never allow a static image or single talking head to exceed 10.5 seconds without a secondary reaction or camera punch-in.

## 5. Agent Execution Prompt Template

When tasked with generating or reviewing a script:
```markdown
1. Hook Score Audit: Rate the opening 3 seconds on a scale of 1-10 for Visceral Curiosity.
2. Cut Fluff: Strip any greeting or preamble.
3. Pattern Interrupt Check: Ensure visual direction changes every 3-4 seconds.
4. Loop Verification: Verify the final sentence seamlessly connects into sentence 1.
```
