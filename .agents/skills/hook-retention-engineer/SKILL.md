# Hook & Retention Engineer

This skill governs audience watch time optimization, swipe-away prevention, and storytelling momentum.

## Core Retention Principles

### 1. The Zero-Fluff Cold Open
- **Never say:** "Welcome back to the channel", "In today's video", "Hey guys", or "Have you ever wondered...".
- **Always start with:** A bold sensory contradiction, an immediate visceral consequence, or high-stakes dilemma.
  - *Example (Shorts):* "If you lived in ancient Rome, you drank spoiled fish guts every single morning—and you paid extra for it."
  - *Example (Longform):* "The crowd is roaring for your blood, but you don't feel like a hero. You are exhausted, starving, and sold for cash."

### 2. The 3.5-Second Pattern Interrupt Rule
- The human brain drops attention after ~3.5 seconds of static visual stimuli.
- Every scene must trigger at least one sensory shift every 3–4 seconds:
  1. Camera motion inversion (zoom-in switches to zoom-out).
  2. Character reaction or gesture change (e.g. from calm explanation to shock/recoil).
  3. Dynamic text highlight color pop.
  4. Ambient SFX or character voice handover.

### 3. The Shorts Seamless Loop
- The final sentence of a Short must syntactically or conceptually bridge into the very first word of Scene 1 without an audible gap.
- Eliminate traditional "outro" cards or "thanks for watching" screens on vertical content.

### 4. Pacing Metrics
- **Optimal Words Per Minute (WPM):** 145–160 WPM.
- **Scene Duration Limit (Shorts):** No single shot should exceed 10.5 seconds without a secondary character cameo or visual focal change.
- **Scene Duration Limit (Longform):** 12–16 seconds per beat with alternating camera motion.
