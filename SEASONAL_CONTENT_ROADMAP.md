## 🔥 Phase 1: Guy Fawkes & Treason Week (Nov 3 – Nov 10)
* **Target Peak:** **November 5 (Bonfire Night)**
* **Cadence:** 8 Days $\times$ 3/day = **24 Shorts**
* **The Hook:** *"Remember, remember, the 5th of November, gunpowder treason and plot..."*
* **Core Topics & Hooks:**
  1. **The 36 Barrels of Death:** How 36 barrels of gunpowder were secretly smuggled under Parliament.
  2. **Guy Fawkes’ Broken Signature:** The brutal torture on the rack in the Tower of London (analyzing his shaky signature before and after).
  3. **Why We Burn Effigies:** The bizarre 400-year British tradition of carrying straw dummies through town squares.
  4. **The Anonymous Mask Origin:** How a 17th-century Catholic rebel's face became the universal symbol of modern hacking and rebellion.
  5. **Tudor/Stuart Executioner Spikes:** Dipping severed heads in boiling pitch and displaying them on London Bridge spikes.

## 🎖️ Phase 2: Wartime Survival & Heroes Week (Nov 11 – Nov 17)
* **Target Peak:** **November 11 (Armistice / Veterans Day)**
* **Cadence:** 7 Days $\times$ 3/day = **21 Shorts**
* **The Hook:** *"At the 11th hour of the 11th day of the 11th month..."*
* **Core Topics & Hooks:**
  1. **Life in WWI Mud Trenches:** Trench foot, chlorine gas masks made of urine-soaked cloth, and cats guarding against giant rats.
  2. **The Ghost Army (WWII):** How 1,100 American artists used inflatable rubber tanks and sound trucks to trick Hitler’s generals.
  3. **Wartime Bomb Defusers:** The suicidal courage of WWII bomb disposal officers listening for ticking delayed-action fuses with stethoscopes.
  4. **Animal War Heroes (Dickin Medals):** Cher Ami the carrier pigeon flying through shrapnel with a severed leg to save 194 soldiers.
  5. **The Spitfire Pilot Reality:** Scrambling four times a day, drinking cold tea in cockpits, and pulling 6G turns over Dover.

## 🦃 Phase 3: The Real 1621 Thanksgiving & Colonial Survival (Nov 18 – Nov 29)
* **Target Peak:** **November 26 (Thanksgiving Day)**
* **Cadence:** 12 Days $\times$ 3/day = **36 Shorts**
* **The Hook:** *"Everything school taught you about the First Thanksgiving is a lie."*
* **Core Topics & Hooks:**
  1. **What They Actually Ate:** No turkey, no mashed potatoes, no pumpkin pie. They ate **wild eels, passenger pigeons, boiled corn mush, and venison**.
  2. **The Starving Winter of 1620:** The brutal Mayflower crossing where half the settlers died of scurvy before planting a single seed.
  3. **Why Pilgrims Drank Beer 3x a Day:** Water was considered contaminated; men, women, and toddlers drank low-alcohol "small beer" from dawn to dusk.
  4. **Squanto’s Impossible Life:** The man who helped the Pilgrims had already been kidnapped to Spain, escaped to London, and learned fluent English before returning home.
  5. **Black Friday’s Bizarre Origins:** The original financial crash of 1869 vs the 1960s Philadelphia police riot designation.

## 👹 Phase 4: Krampus & Alpine Dark Winter Folklore (Dec 1 – Dec 10)
* **Target Peak:** **December 5 (Krampusnacht)**
* **Cadence:** 10 Days $\times$ 3/day = **30 Shorts**
* **The Hook:** *"Santa brings toys... but his horned shadow takes bad children to hell."*
* **Core Topics & Hooks:**
  1. **The Legend of Krampusnacht:** Why Alpine Austrian villages sent masked demons into streets with whips and chains on December 5.
  2. **The Birch Switch & Wicker Basket:** The terrifying punishment where naughty children were stuffed into a basket on Krampus's back.
  3. **Frau Perchta (The Belly-Slitter):** The ancient winter witch who slit open the stomachs of lazy spinners and stuffed them with straw.
  4. **The Yule Cat (Jólakötturinn):** The giant Icelandic feline that prowls frozen snowfields devouring anyone who didn't receive new wool clothes before Christmas.

## 🏛️ Phase 5: Roman Saturnalia, Viking Yule & Victorian Christmas (Dec 13 – Dec 31)
* **Target Peak:** **December 17–25 (Saturnalia / Winter Solstice / Yule)**
* **Cadence:** 19 Days $\times$ 3/day = **57 Shorts**
* **The Hook:** *"The craziest holiday in human history where slaves ruled their masters."*
* **Core Topics & Hooks:**
  1. **Roman Saturnalia (Dec 17–23):** Masters served banquets to their enslaved household, dice gambling was legalized in the forum, and senators dressed in wild party caps.
  2. **Viking Yule & The Great Blót:** 12 days of winter feasting, animal sacrifices, and trench fires to ward off hungry winter draugr and mountain trolls.
  3. **Victorian Ghost Stories on Christmas Eve:** Why Victorians spent Christmas Eve telling spine-chilling ghost stories by candlelight (*A Christmas Carol*).
  4. **The Christmas Truce of 1914:** German and British soldiers singing Silent Night across No Man's Land and playing soccer in the frost.
  5. **Ancient New Year & The God Janus:** Roman superstitions for the two-faced god of doorways and beginnings on January 1.
